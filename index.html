FAQ

Questions

Is ThumbGen really free?

Yes. The public beta is free with every feature. ThumbGen is invite-only while it grows, and a Pro version is coming later. Beta users will get early access.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

New programs without a paid code-signing certificate trigger Microsoft SmartScreen. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only have to do this once.

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every AI model runs on your own computer. The internet is only used to download the models the first time, and to fetch a YouTube thumbnail if you paste a link.

Where are the AI models stored? How do I uninstall?

Models and settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ThumbGen. To uninstall, delete ThumbGen.exe and that folder. To keep everything next to the exe instead, put an empty portable.txt beside it.

Does it need a graphics card?

No. The beta runs on the CPU, so it works on almost any PC. GPU acceleration is planned.

Can I use the thumbnails on a monetised channel?

The images you make are yours, but the AI models have their own licences. SD-Turbo and SDXL-Turbo use Stability AI's community licence, which limits commercial use for larger businesses. Check it before using AI-generated backgrounds commercially.
